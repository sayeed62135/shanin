02 — FAQ

Common Questions

What is Shanin Group's minimum order quantity?

With a monthly capacity of 210,000 pieces across 26 sewing lines, we accommodate both smaller trial orders and large ongoing programmes. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements and timeline.

What products do you manufacture?

We specialise in woven ready-made garments: men's formal and casual shirts, ladies blouses, school shirts and blouses, boys' casual shirts, and men's and boys' casual trousers. In-house embroidery is also available.

What quality standards do you apply?

We operate AQL 2.5 as standard, with AQL 1.5 available for buyers requiring stricter tolerances. We use a dual QC system — factory-internal and head office buying team — plus 100% metal detection on all finished products.

Can we visit your factory?

Absolutely — we welcome factory visits from buyers and sourcing teams. Please contact Mr. Faruk Al Nasir (CEO) at faruk@shaningroup.net or via the form above to arrange a visit to our Dhaka and Gazipur facilities.

Where do you source your fabric from?

We source fabrics from premier mills across Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, China, India, Taiwan, and Korea — covering cotton, T/C, CVC, polynosics, and polyviscose micro fabrics. All incoming fabric is inspected using the 4-Point system.

Are you a BGMEA-registered manufacturer?

Yes. Shanin Group is an active member of BGMEA (Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers & Exporters Association), BKMEA, FBCCI, and operates units registered with BEPZA at Adamjee EPZ, Narayangonj.
