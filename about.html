01 — Company Overview

Who We Are

“Shanin Group is a leading garment manufacturer committed to producing high-quality apparel for international markets — built on three decades of excellence.”

Shanin Group specialises in a wide range of ready-made garments, including woven and denim products, catering to some of the most renowned global brands. The company operates state-of-the-art production facilities equipped with advanced machinery and modern technology, ensuring precision, efficiency, and consistency at every stage.

Sustainability and ethical manufacturing are at the heart of our operations — we prioritise eco-friendly processes, safe working environments, and fair labour practices. A highly skilled workforce, supported by experienced management, drives continuous improvement in quality and productivity.

Through our focus on customer satisfaction, timely delivery, and competitive pricing, Shanin Group has earned the trust of clients worldwide. Our vision is to become a global leader in the garment industry by blending craftsmanship with innovation and maintaining strong business ethics.

02 — Company History

Our Journey
Since 1991

1991
Founding
Shanin Group is Born

Shanin Group was established in 1990–1991 as a ready-made garments (RMG) company in Bangladesh. The company began with a small-scale sewing operation, focusing on woven product manufacturing. The Shanin Corporation Limited, the flagship production unit, was incorporated with 8 sewing lines at Pilkhana, Dhaka.

1994
Expansion
Alpha Knitting Wear Ltd. Established

The group expanded into knitwear manufacturing with the establishment of Alpha Knitting Wear Ltd. in 1994, broadening its product range and manufacturing expertise beyond woven garments.

2001
Growth
The Shanin Clothing Limited Launches

The Shanin Clothing Limited was established in 2001, adding a dedicated unit for boys' woven shirts with 5 production lines in Dhaka. This brought the group's total capacity significantly higher and introduced specialised children's garment manufacturing.

2000s
Vertical Integration
Building a Complete Supply Chain

Shanin Group invested in vertical integration, establishing Fem Interlining Ltd. (Adamjee EPZ), Fem Accessories Ltd., Shanin Packages Ltd., and Shan Embroidery Ltd. — creating a fully in-house supply chain covering interlining, accessories, packaging, and embroidery.

2010s
Modernisation
Hanger System Automation Adopted

Shanin Group adopted the overhead hanger/garment handling system — a mechanised overhead rail system with barcode/RFID tracking, automatic line balancing, and real-time WIP monitoring — dramatically improving production efficiency, quality traceability, and output speed across its sewing lines.

Today
Present Day
34+ Years of Manufacturing Excellence

Shanin Group now operates 8 specialised production units with 26 sewing lines, 8,300+ employees, a daily output of 45,000 pieces, and an annual turnover of approximately US$ 70 million. The UK represents approximately 65% of total production, with clients including Marks & Spencer, Primark, Next, Tesco, and 30+ other global brands.

04 — Fabric Division

Global Fabric
Sourcing

“Our fabric division sources from the finest mills across Asia, ensuring consistent quality, colour continuity, and on-time availability for every production run.”

Shanin Group maintains strong relationships with premier fabric mills across 7 countries, enabling us to source a wide variety of shirting fabrics to meet every buyer specification. All incoming fabric undergoes strict 4-Point inspection before entering production.

Fabric is stored in a cool, dry environment on pallets, with colour continuity cards maintained roll-wise. A 100% numbering system is applied to all fabric parts during production to ensure full shade control and traceability throughout the manufacturing process.

05 — Memberships

Industry Associations

BGMEA
Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers & Exporters Association

Active member of BGMEA, the primary industry body for Bangladesh's export-oriented RMG sector. Membership demonstrates compliance with ethical manufacturing, labour, and export standards.

BKMEA
Bangladesh Knitwear Manufacturers & Exporters Association

Member of BKMEA, reflecting Shanin Group's involvement in knitwear manufacturing through Alpha Knitting Wear Ltd. and demonstrating breadth across both woven and knit garment categories.

Affiliated with FBCCI, the apex trade body of Bangladesh, confirming Shanin Group's standing as a reputable, compliant business entity contributing to the national economy and export sector.
