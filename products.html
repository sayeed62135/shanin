02 — Standards

Production
Standards

“Every piece leaving our factory has passed through a rigorous dual quality control system — our promise to every buyer.”

Shanin Group applies AQL 2.5 as standard across all production — with AQL 1.5 available for buyers requiring stricter tolerances. Our 30-week training programme for production personnel ensures a consistently skilled workforce. All workers must be a minimum of 18 years of age.

Before bulk production begins, approximately 100 pieces are trial-cut and inspected. Metal detector scanning is applied to 100% of finished products. Generator backup ensures uninterrupted production across all units for up to 24 hours continuously.
