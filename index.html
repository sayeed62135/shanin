01 — About Us

Built on Trust,
Driven by Precision

"Three decades of craftsmanship have shaped us into one of Bangladesh's most trusted ready-made garment manufacturers."

Shanin Group was founded in 1991 with a clear mission: to produce world-class apparel through a combination of skilled craftsmanship, modern technology, and unwavering commitment to quality. Today, we operate eight specialised production units with over 8,300 employees, serving premier global brands across Europe and the United States.

Our integrated manufacturing model — spanning fabric sourcing, cutting, sewing, embroidery, finishing, and packaging — allows us to maintain exceptional quality control at every step, delivering consistent results that our clients depend on season after season.

02 — Capabilities

End-to-End
Manufacturing Expertise

01
Fabric Sourcing
Strategic partnerships with premier mills across Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, China, India, Taiwan, and Korea — sourcing cotton, T/C, CVC, polynosics, and polyviscose micro fabrics.
7 Source Countries
02
Cutting Department
25,000 pieces per day cutting capacity with manual and band knife equipment. 4-Point fabric inspection, 200-layer normal laying size, shade-controlled lot-wise ticketing system.
25,000 pcs / day
03
Production & Sewing
26 production lines across 8 specialised units with advanced hanger system automation — real-time WIP tracking, automatic line balancing, and barcode/RFID-enabled workflow control.
26 Sewing Lines
04
Quality Assurance
Dual quality teams: factory-internal team with 100% line-end and finishing inspection, and a head office buying team conducting pre-final at AQL 2.5. Metal detector on all products.
AQL 2.5 / 1.5 Standard
05
Sample Room
Dedicated sample room producing 150–200 pieces per day covering development, sealing, pre-production, and styling approval samples — 80% in-house, 20% on production floor.
150–200 pcs / day
06
Accessories & Packaging
Vertically integrated with in-house interlining (Fem Interlining), accessories (Fem Accessories), and packaging (Shanin Packages) — 50 export carton sets per day, all labels, hangtags, and poly bags.
Fully Vertically Integrated
