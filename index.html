01 — About Us

Built on Trust,
Driven by Precision

“Over three decades of craftsmanship have shaped us into one of Bangladesh's most trusted ready-made garment manufacturers.”

Founded in 1991 with a clear mission: to produce world-class apparel through skilled craftsmanship, modern technology, and unwavering quality. Today, Shanin Group operates eight specialised production units with over 8,300 employees, serving premier global brands across Europe and the United States.

Our integrated manufacturing model — spanning fabric sourcing from 7 countries, precision cutting, sewing across 26 lines, embroidery, finishing, and packaging — ensures exceptional quality control at every stage, season after season.

End-to-End
Manufacturing Expertise

Fabric Sourcing

Strategic partnerships with premier mills across Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, China, India, Taiwan, and Korea — sourcing cotton, T/C, CVC, polynosics, and polyviscose micro fabrics with 4-Point inspection on all lots.

04 — Quality

Precision at
Every Step

Shanin Group maintains a complete internal quality policy with two dedicated QC teams: a factory-internal team conducting 100% inspection at line-end and after ironing, and a head office buying team running pre-final inspections at AQL 2.5 before every buyer invitation. All products pass metal detector checks.

01

Fabric Inspection (4-Point)

All incoming fabric rolls inspected by 4-Point system via inspection machine and visual inspection during lay. Color continuity cards maintained roll-wise.

02

In-Line Quality Control

6 Production Inspectors per line conducting hourly lot inspections. Records maintained every hour against sealed sample and head office approval samples.

03

PP Meeting & Head Office QC

Pre-Production (PP) Meeting held with factory QC team and management. Head office QC Manager gives internal approval based on sealed/PP samples and comments. At least 2–3 QCs stationed per factory under DP Managers and Assistant Managers.

04

Pre-Final & Final Inspection

Own Pre-Final Inspection at AQL 2.5 before buyer invitation. QC Manager-led final inspection. Trial cut of ~100 pieces before bulk production commences.

05

Metal Detection & Needle Policy

100% metal detector scanning of all finished products. Broken needle policy per UK buyer standards with full record-keeping for every case.

AQL

AQL 2.5 / 1.5

Standard AQL 2.5 applied on all orders; AQL 1.5 available for buyers with stricter requirements.

BGMEA

Industry Member

Active member of Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers & Exporters Association — verified ethical manufacturer.

24H

Generator Backup

Generator facility available in all production units for 24-hour uninterrupted power supply.

30W

Training Programme

30-week structured training period for all production personnel. Minimum worker age strictly 18 years.
